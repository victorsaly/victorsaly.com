# victorsaly.com: home and work pages

Scope: the home page (`/`) and each project page (`/work/<slug>`). Visitor mode: Experience. The visual world is
blog.victorsaly.com's, fixed: tokens, Atkinson Hyperlegible with the Lexend switch, ink rules in three weights,
marigold on titles, controls as bordered boxes. Audience: clients and employers deciding whether to work with
Victor. Projects only; the day job gets one line saying the work is separate from it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is a printed catalogue of plates. Each project gets one full-width plate (its real screen, its
number, its title, its message, a facts table) so a client reads the evidence, not claims. It refuses the
category default: a hero with a tagline, then a grid of rounded cards with hover lifts.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, black ink, one marigold mark per plate on the title. Heavy 8px rule opens the masthead
and closes the page; strong 3px rule opens each plate; hairlines between facts rows. Screens sit in a 1px ink
frame, no radius, no shadow. Numbers quiet and tabular. Atkinson throughout.

STORY: A visitor sees whose work this is, then plate after plate of real things that run, each with what it
does, what it is built with, and two plain ways in: use it, read the source. They believe he finishes, and they
open one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desk: heavy rule; small label "Work"; "Victor Saly" large; one line saying what the work is;
count and the two links (GitHub, email) in quiet type. Plate 01 starts inside the fold: screen in the left
seven columns, at right its number, title in marigold, message, and the facts table with Use it and Source as
controls. Phone: masthead, then plate 01's screen full width with its title below.

CATALOGUE APPARATUS (added 2026-11-21, after the first critique): like a printed catalogue, it has a contents
page and sections. Under the masthead, "The apps, by kind" lists every project in four ruled columns, live ones
numbered; on a phone only the four kinds show, as jump links. The plates
follow in sections by kind (For clients and customers, Games, For my kids, AI tools), each opened by its label's
strong rule. Only finished projects with a live link and a screenshot are listed. Plate numbers run straight through. A plate's facts table may
carry "Made for" (client, customers, team) and says "Private" when the source is private. Screens are in colour
everywhere (changed 2026-11-21: the apps are colourful, and grey hid what kind each was). On a phone the first viewport now ends on the
contents' kinds rather than plate 01: the contents earns that place.

MORE LIFE (added 2026-11-21, asked for because the page read as plain): the masthead carries the blog's
linocut portrait print beside the name (in dark mode it keeps its paper, dimmed, as the blog does). Under the
masthead, a cover: every screen small, in its hairline frame with its number, two rows on a desk and one row to
swipe on a phone, each jumping to its plate; it is decorative for assistive technology, since the contents says
the same in words. The page's one authored motion: a plate with a film plays six silent seconds of it (a cut
preview, not the film) while a fine pointer is on the plate, fading in only once it really plays; never on touch
or with reduced motion. Links to the apps and their source open in a new tab.

FORM: Specimen plates, number 2 of 7 on my list, seed 6789f5f0.
