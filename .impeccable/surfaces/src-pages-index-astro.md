# victorsaly.com: home and work pages

Scope: the home page (`/`) and each project page (`/work/<slug>`). Visitor mode: Experience. The visual world is
blog.victorsaly.com's, fixed: tokens, Atkinson Hyperlegible with the Lexend switch, ink rules in three weights,
marigold on titles, controls as bordered boxes. Audience: clients and employers deciding whether to work with
Victor. Projects only; no day job on the site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is a printed catalogue of plates. Each project gets one full-width plate (its real screen, its
number, its title, its message, a facts table) so a client reads the evidence, not claims. It refuses the
category default: a hero with a tagline, then a grid of rounded cards with hover lifts.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, black ink, one marigold mark per plate on the title. Heavy 8px rule opens the masthead
and closes the page; strong 3px rule opens each plate; hairlines between facts rows. Screens sit in a 1px ink
frame, no radius, no shadow. Numbers quiet and tabular. Atkinson throughout.

STORY: A visitor sees whose work this is, then plate after plate of real things that run, each with what it
does, what it is built with, and two plain ways in: use it, read the source. They believe he finishes, and they
open one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desk: heavy rule; small label "Work"; "Victor Saly" large; one line saying what the work is;
count and the two links (GitHub, email) in quiet type. Plate 01 starts inside the fold: screen in the left
seven columns, at right its number, title in marigold, message, and the facts table with Use it and Source as
controls. Phone: masthead, then plate 01's screen full width with its title below.

FORM: Specimen plates, number 2 of 7 on my list, seed 6789f5f0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
